About Tamien

Tamien Dysart is a leadership speaker, culture architect, and transformation strategist. He helps organizations build intentional cultures and develop stronger leaders through Think 3D Solutions, and helps individuals elevate their personal leadership through Elevate You. He speaks at corporate events, conferences, retreats, and nonprofit organizations across the United States.

Tamien is based in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. He speaks and consults nationally across the United States, working with corporate organizations, nonprofits, chambers of commerce, healthcare systems, and educational institutions.

As site director at Capital One, Tamien reduced employee turnover from 33% to 6% in just 18 months through personal development investment and intentional culture-building — well below the industry average. His Leaders of Tomorrow program clients consistently reach 80–90%+ employee engagement within two years. He has delivered over 100 speaking events across industries and published two books: Think 3D and Elevate: Elevate Your Thinking, Elevate Your Life (2023).

Think 3D & Elevate You

Think 3D Solutions is an organizational culture consulting and leadership development company. Its flagship program is Leaders of Tomorrow (LOT) — a 12-week accredited leadership development cohort with more than 1,000 graduates. Think 3D works with organizations using the Mindset → Skillset → Culture Set framework to curate elevated, intentional workplace cultures.

Elevate You is a personal development platform and behavioral installation system built on the 4 A's framework — Awareness, Alignment, Activation, and Amplification. Programs include the Power of M.E. retreat, the E.L.I.T.E. Mindset 2-day retreat, and the Author of My Life (AOML) 90-day cohort program. The core belief: when you elevate the individual, you amplify the whole.

Think 3D Solutions focuses on the organizational level — culture consulting, manager development, leadership pipelines, and corporate training. Elevate You focuses on the individual level — personal development retreats, self-leadership frameworks, and behavioral installation for high-achieving individuals. Together they form Tamien's Integrated Ripple Effect Ecosystem: elevate the individual, evolve the culture, extend opportunity to the community.

Leaders of Tomorrow (LOT) is Think 3D Solutions' flagship 12-week leadership development cohort. It is structured, accredited, and has more than 1,000 graduates. The program develops emerging and mid-level leaders within organizations, building a leadership pipeline through the Mindset → Skillset → Culture Set framework.

Frameworks & Methodology

The 4 A's is Tamien's core transformation framework used across all programs: Awareness (who am I — self-knowledge and current state), Alignment (values, definitions of success, what you actually want), Activation (committed habits, routines, and measurable practice), and Amplification (purpose, ripple effect, and impact beyond self). It is the foundation of Elevate You and is woven throughout Think 3D's organizational programs.
