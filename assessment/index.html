Before you begin

Rate each of the 30 areas from 1 to 10. Tap Learn more under any area for a plain-language description, questions to ask yourself, and what each score looks like.

1–3Depleting
4–6Maintenance
7–8Intentional
9–10Elite
  1. Rate your reality, not your intentions. Score what you actually do.
  2. Think about the last 30 days. Your typical, ordinary days.
  3. Low scores are not failures. A 2 is a starting point, not a grade.
  4. Watch for the "5 trap." If you land on 5, ask: is it truly average, or am I avoiding the truth?
  5. Ask someone who knows you if you are unsure. Others often see what we cannot.
