About Tamien

Tamien Dysart brings more than 16+ years of leadership experience inside Fortune 500 environments and the last decade as an entrepreneur helping organizations intentionally elevate culture. He has worked with high-growth Inc. 5000 companies focused on sustainability, as well as established organizations with good cultures that were ready to move from good to great.
