About Tamien

I Didn't Learn
Leadership.
I Decided It.

Fifteen years in corporate America taught me one thing clearly: most leaders are winging it. Including me, for a while.

Then a $7.99 John Maxwell book at Sam's Club changed everything.

I went from watching what not to do on a credit card collections floor to becoming a site director at Capital One — where we reduced employee turnover from 33% to 6% in 18 months. Not by adding a perk. By changing what people came home feeling like.

That result didn't come from a program. It came from a decision to invest in culture with the same seriousness that organizations invest in strategy.

I've spent the years since turning that insight into a system — for leaders, for organizations, and for the next generation trying to build something real before the rocking chair. Nothing scares me more than unintentional living. So I don't do it. And I help others stop, too.
